Form a new herd

Select a male foal or young stallion when you decide he should leave his birth herd. This does not change his ancestry or breeding allowances.

Breeding & foaling

Pregnancies and parentage are saved. Each horse earns one breeding allowance at levels 10, 20, 30, and so on; unused allowances carry forward. Full and half siblings, and parents with offspring, cannot breed. Record foaling when it happens in AVH.

Known ancestry for an existing horse

Random events

Each event randomly chooses a horse or an established stallion band. Level-gain events increase the chosen horse by exactly one level (record the new level after updating AVH). Challenges may target one mare (70%) or a whole herd (30%). If a breeding event selects a horse too young to breed, simply reroll.

Weights: Level gains 65% · Challenges 15% · Acquisitions 10% · Herd events 8% · Breeding 2%. Training events increase one randomly selected horse by one level.

Stallion challenge roller

The higher-level stallion gains +1 per complete 10-level difference. Ties are rerolled. Challenge results automatically transfer mares. Retired defenders automatically lose. A defeated herd leader becomes a bachelor and can challenge again.
